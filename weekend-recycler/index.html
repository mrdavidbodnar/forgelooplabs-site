The core product concept

Weekend Recycler Desktop Filament Recycling System

Weekend Recycler is a desktop system under development to process clean 3D-print waste into reusable 1.75 mm filament.

A compact, integrated recycling workflow

Weekend Recycler is being designed around four connected stages: shredding clean failed prints, extruding the prepared material, cooling the filament strand and winding it onto a reusable spool.

Designed for makers

The intended product is a desktop-scale system for people who regularly accumulate failed prints, supports and other clean print waste.

Targeting 1.75 mm filament

The current concept targets the filament diameter commonly used by consumer and prosumer material-extrusion printers.

Prototype first

The V0 build prioritizes visible mechanics, manual control and measurable testing before consumer features are added.

Development status: Weekend Recycler is under development and is not currently offered for sale. Final capabilities and specifications require engineering validation.
