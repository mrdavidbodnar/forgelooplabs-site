Four-stage recycling workflow

How 3D-Print Waste Becomes New Filament

Learn how clean failed 3D prints can be shredded, extruded, cooled and wound into reusable filament in a desktop recycling workflow.

01

Shred

Clean failed prints and supports are reduced into smaller, inspectable pieces suitable for controlled feeding.

02

Extrude

A heated screw system melts and meters prepared material through a replaceable die.

03

Cool

An adjustable cooling path stabilizes the emerging strand before it reaches the puller.

04

Spool

A controlled puller and low-tension winding stage collect the new filament.

Why control matters

Recycled filament depends on consistent material preparation, temperature, feed rate, cooling and pull speed. The prototype separates the stages so each variable can be observed and improved during testing.

Material compatibility and repeatability will be reported only after controlled trials. Clean, correctly sorted input material is essential; mixed or contaminated plastics can produce unreliable results.
