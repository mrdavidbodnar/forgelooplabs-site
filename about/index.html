About ForgeLoop Labs Inc.

Learn about ForgeLoop Labs Inc., an Alberta product-development company creating desktop recycling technology for 3D-printing waste.

Building a practical path from scrap to spool

ForgeLoop Labs Inc. is an independent product-development company based in Edson, Alberta. The company is developing the Weekend Recycler, a compact desktop system intended to convert clean failed 3D prints and printing waste into reusable 1.75 mm filament.

The project is currently in prototype development. Public information focuses on the mission, product concept and development progress while detailed drawings, supplier information, dimensions and fabrication methods remain private.

A distinct company and project

ForgeLoop Labs Inc. is not affiliated with similarly named software projects or unrelated rapid-prototyping companies. Weekend Recycler is the company’s desktop filament-recovery project.
